# Owatonna Reel — Gemini Still Prompts

Case: Mrs. Ralph Butler / "the Owatonna Visitor," Nov 1966 – May 1967.
Target: ~18-19s reel, 4 content beats + existing end card (Vance
portrait, no new generation needed). 2 bonus stills included for the
carousel that follows.

**Note on likeness:** Mrs. Butler and "Major Richard French" are
anonymous figures from 1960s rural folklore — no real photograph of
either is known to exist (unlike, say, Betty and Barney Hill, who do
have documented real photos). These are fully generated, generic
period-appropriate figures, not an attempt to recreate any specific
real photo. If you have a preferred face/actor style for either
figure, mention it and I'll adjust the prompts to keep them consistent
across all stills.

All prompts: vertical 9:16, photorealistic, cinematic film-still
quality, natural film grain, no text/watermarks/logos in frame.

---

## Beat 1 — The Sighting
> A cinematic film still, vertical 9:16. A rural Minnesota field at
> night, November 1966. Two women in their 40s stand close together in
> the tall grass, backs mostly to camera, looking toward the far end
> of the field. In the distance, several small orange-white lights
> hover low over the ground, one noticeably lower and closer than the
> others. The sky above is deep navy with faint stars, the field lit
> only by moonlight and the glow of the lights. Cold, still atmosphere.
> Photorealistic, shot on film, slight grain, muted natural colors,
> no text.

## Beat 2 — The Trance
> A cinematic film still, vertical 9:16, close-up. A woman in her 40s
> in a plain 1960s housedress, kneeling in tall grass at night, caught
> mid-trance — eyes half-lidded and unfocused, mouth slightly open as
> if speaking, face lit from one side by a soft warm-orange glow from
> just outside the frame. Her posture is rigid, unnatural stillness.
> Behind her, out of focus, a second woman watches with visible alarm.
> Photorealistic, shot on film, shallow depth of field, slight grain,
> no text.

## Beat 3 — The Visitor
> A cinematic film still, vertical 9:16. A man in his late 30s stands
> on the front step of a modest wood-frame farmhouse at dusk, May
> 1967. He wears a brand-new, slightly ill-fitting gray suit, white
> shirt, black tie — clothes that look unworn, almost stiff. His
> expression is flat, formally polite, faintly awkward. Behind him in
> the gravel driveway, a white 1960s Ford Mustang is parked at an
> odd angle. Warm porch light spills onto him from an unseen doorway
> just out of frame. Photorealistic, shot on film, dusk color
> temperature, slight grain, no text.

## Beat 4 — The Jello
> A cinematic film still, vertical 9:16, close-up. The same man in the
> gray suit sits at a modest 1960s kitchen table, holding a glass
> bowl of red Jello up to his mouth as if to drink it like a cup,
> tilting it, clearly unfamiliar with how to eat it — no spoon in
> hand. His expression is earnest and slightly confused, not comedic
> or mugging for camera. Warm kitchen lighting, checkered tablecloth
> visible at the edge of frame. Photorealistic, shot on film, slight
> grain, no text.

## Production notes for later
- Music: `mystery-tension` (mood: curiosity) — matches both the
  sighting's unease and the MIB visit's slow-building strangeness.
- Target length: ~18-19s (4 beats × ~4.5s + ~3s end card). Can trim to
  4s/beat (~19s total) if needed to fit exactly 15-20s.
- End card: reuse Vance's existing portrait/CTA composite pattern from
  prior reels — no new generation needed for that beat.
- Once stills are back: standard pre-flight (resolution ≥1080px
  short side, corners checked for watermarks, likeness/consistency
  check across all 4-6 images since they need to read as the same two
  people throughout).
