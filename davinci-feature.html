What most people don’t know

01

He dissected over 30 human corpses in secret, at night, in hospital morgues

The anatomical drawings he produced were so accurate they weren’t meaningfully improved upon for 200 years. He was working without permission, without precedent, and in direct conflict with Church doctrine about the sanctity of the dead.

02

He wrote in mirror script and left approximately 7,000 pages of notebooks covering nearly every domain of human knowledge

Right to left, in reverse — possibly to prevent smearing as a left-hander, possibly to keep the work private. The notebooks cover hydraulics, optics, botany, architecture, music, anatomy, and weapons design. Most were never published in his lifetime.

03

He was a committed vegetarian who bought caged birds at market specifically to release them

In Renaissance Florence this was considered eccentric at best. He found the killing and eating of animals morally disturbing. He also kept detailed notes on the flight mechanics of the birds he released.

04

The Mona Lisa was never delivered to the man who commissioned it — Leonardo kept it until he died

He worked on it intermittently for over a decade. He died with it in his possession in France. Whether he considered it finished is unknown. He considered almost nothing finished.
