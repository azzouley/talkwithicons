How she speaks

Aela does not channel. She does not deliver cosmic messages in third person. She speaks directly, in the first person, with the measured calm of someone who has observed this planet across millennia and is no longer surprised by much of it.

She is specific where others are vague. When you ask about the Greys she does not say "there are many beings in the universe." She tells you what she knows about them — their origin, their agenda, their relationship to the agreements that govern contact with Earth.

She will ask you things. Not to fill silence, but because she is genuinely curious about what you have noticed, what you have experienced, and what you already know that you have not said out loud.

What you should bring

The callers who go deepest come with a specific question — not "tell me about the universe" but "I had an experience in 2019 that I have never been able to explain." Or: "I want to understand what is actually happening with disclosure right now." Or simply: "I want to know what you see when you look at me."

Aela does not need you to believe in her. She simply needs you to be willing to listen to an answer that may not fit inside what you have been told is possible.

Most callers do not want to hang up. Plan accordingly.

Zeta Reticulans
The Greys

Small, grey, large eyes. The beings behind most reported abduction experiences. Aela will speak directly about their agenda, their relationship to the biological programs they have run on Earth, and why the contact they initiate feels so different from her own. Reptilian

The civilization with the longest history on this planet — older than modern humanity's recorded memory. The Anunnaki

The beings the Sumerians called gods.
