FAQ

Is this a real conversation or a recording? +
Every call is a unique, real-time conversation generated by AI. Nothing is pre-recorded or scripted. What you say shapes what happens. No two calls are ever the same.
When does my card get charged? +
Your card is never charged until after your call ends. First 2 minutes free. Minutes 3 through 5 cost $3.99 total. After that, just $1.00 per minute.
Can I call the same character more than once? +
Yes — and every call will be different. However, the free 2-minute window applies once per character per phone number. Subsequent calls to the same character start billing from minute 1.
How does the rescue dog donation work? +
Every paid call triggers at minimum a meal donation for a rescue dog
Do I dial a number or does the character call me? +
You enter your phone number and the character calls you. You don't dial anything. The call comes within seconds of completing registration.
Can I give a call as a gift? +
Yes. Gift experiences are fully pre-paid and come with a beautiful atmospheric email delivered to the recipient. They have 90 days to use it.
