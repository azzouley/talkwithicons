October 1957, rural Brazil. Antônio Villas-Boas was ploughing at night to avoid the heat when a light came down over his field. By his account his tractor died and he was taken aboard a craft, where beings drew his blood. He told the same story until he died in 1991. Arthur Vance calls it the case that came before anyone used the word abduction.
