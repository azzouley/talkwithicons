## FACT CHECK — done before anything else, all confirmed accurate
- Date: November 5, 1975.
- Location: Turkey Springs unit, Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, near
  Heber, Arizona.
- Crew: seven men total — Travis Walton plus Mike Rogers, Ken Peterson,
  John Goulette, Steve Pierce, Allan (also spelled Allen) Dalis, and
  Dwayne Smith.
- Phone call: Walton called from a gas station pay phone in Heber on
  November 10, 1975, five days after the crew fled the scene.
- Polygraphs: administered the same day, November 10, 1975, by Cy Gilson
  of the Arizona Department of Public Safety, to the six remaining
  crewmen. Five passed. Allan Dalis's result was recorded inconclusive —
  Gilson attributed this to lack of cooperation, not deception.
- No claim in the brief overclaims the case as proven, and none states a
  conclusion about what the object was. Verified consistent with source
  reporting (Wikipedia's Travis Walton incident entry, cross-referenced
  against multiple case summaries).

## STEP 1 — STILL INSPECTION RESULTS

### Still 1 — Clearing: PASSES, with one flag
- Craft reads as a smooth-hulled disc with a lit horizontal midsection
  band — does not resemble the 1993 Paramount film's design. Correct.
- No clearly rendered faces — all figures shown from behind or at
  distance. Correct.
- Period-plausible truck and workwear, no modern elements visible.
- Vertical 9:16, consistent grain/color grade.
- **Flag: headcount.** Only 5 people are visible (4 standing by the truck
  + 1 walking toward the craft) against a required 7-man crew (6 remaining
  + Walton). This is the exact failure mode documented from an earlier
  session on this same case (a prior Travis Walton still was rejected for
  showing 4 loggers instead of 6, in a synchronized/posed arrangement) —
  flagging with that history in mind even though this composition itself
  isn't posed or absurd, just short two people.

### Still 2 — Logging road: FAILS, two independent grounds
- **Multiple clearly-rendered, forward-facing human faces.** Zoomed
  inspection of the truck bed shows 3 men sitting facing the camera (one,
  center, in a red flannel shirt, is rendered in enough detail to show a
  full beard and expression) plus 2 more visible through the cab's rear
  window — 5 faces with real detail, not silhouettes or figures turned
  away. This is the core real-person-likeness risk the brief exists to
  prevent.
- **The background "residual glow" is a defined craft shape, not diffuse
  light.** Zoomed inspection of the gap in the treeline shows a distinct
  disc silhouette with a bright lit band — essentially the same craft from
  Still 1, still fully legible as a UFO — not the "diffuse residual glow,
  no defined craft shape" the brief specifies.
- Also short on headcount: 5 visible vs. 6 required (Travis already
  separated from the group at this point in the story).

### Still 3 — Phone booth: FAILS
- **A clearly rendered face in profile** — visible eye, nose, mouth,
  jawline, and hairstyle in enough detail to read as a specific person's
  likeness. Given the unambiguous context (a lit phone booth, a gas
  station, this exact case), this is squarely the "no likeness, in any
  scene" the brief prohibits, and Travis Walton is a real, living,
  commercially active person.
- Secondary, non-blocking note: the pose is standing and alert (one arm
  braced on the glass, mid-conversation) rather than "slumped," as
  specified.
- Passes: no craft or sky lights present; period-plausible booth, pumps,
  and signage.

## DECISION
Per the brief's own instruction ("If a still fails, stop and report which
one and why — do not proceed with it and do not attempt to fix it in
motion"): stopped here. Stills 2 and 3 need to be regenerated without
rendering recognizable faces (Travis Walton's likeness risk applies to
all three men who'd be shown, not just Walton, but the profile face in
Still 3 is unambiguously meant to be him). Still 2 additionally needs the
background craft reworked into genuinely diffuse glow. Still 1 is usable
but would benefit from 1-2 more crew members near the truck if a
regeneration pass is happening anyway.
