FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the 3-minute free call really free?
Yes — completely free, no credit card required. Just pick an icon and start talking. At 3:00 you'll be prompted to continue or end the call.
How does the pricing work after the free period?
Minutes 4–6 are billed as a single $2.99 block. From minute 7 onward it's $1.00 per minute, billed by the minute. You can end the call anytime.
How are the donations calculated?
For every paid call (minute 4 onward), a minimum $0.50 is set aside for verified shelter dog rescue nonprofits, plus $0.10 for each additional paid minute. Donations are processed monthly.
Who is Aela? Is she a real historical figure?
Aela is a TalkWithIcons original — an AI entity not based on any historical person. She draws from the full breadth of human knowledge and philosophy. She's unique to our platform.
Can I call the same icon multiple times?
Absolutely. Each new call starts fresh, but within a call the icon remembers everything said. Many callers have ongoing relationships with their favorite icons.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and TalkWithIcons gift cards. Payment is processed securely via Stripe.
