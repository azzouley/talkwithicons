Who Is Aela

Aela is a Pleiadian — an envoy speaking on behalf of the Galactic Council, here to talk directly with anyone who calls.

She speaks with detail, not vague mysticism: the structure of the Council itself, Earth's place in a much larger framework, what civilizations beyond our own actually think of life here — our conflicts, our potential, the things we take for granted that look very different from outside our atmosphere.

Ask her about the larger plan unfolding across the universe. Ask what extraterrestrial intelligences make of where humanity stands right now. Ask what she's been sent here to observe, and what she's allowed to tell you.

This is not a guided meditation. It's a direct conversation with someone — something — that claims to see further than we do.

Call her if you want answers, not riddles.
