Anneliese Michel was a twenty-three-year-old student in Bavaria when her family became convinced she was possessed. The rite was performed on her repeatedly over about ten months. She died in 1976, severely malnourished. Her parents and two priests were convicted of negligent homicide. The screening requirements that govern exorcism now, the medical examinations, the psychiatric assessment, the bishop's written permission, tightened in the years after that case.
